# Math Sessions 1–2 — Formula & Recognition Sheet

## Straight Lines

m = (y2-y1)/(x2-x1)

y = mx+c

y-y1 = m(x-x1)

Ax+By+C=0 → m=-A/B when B≠0.

Parallel: m1=m2.

Perpendicular: m1*m2=-1.

Horizontal: y=k.

Vertical: x=k.

## Circle

(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2

Centre (h,k), radius r.

## Parabola

(y-k)^2=4a(x-h)

Focus (h+a,k), directrix x=h-a.

(x-h)^2=4a(y-k)

Focus (h,k+a), directrix y=k-a.

Latus rectum length = 4|a|.

## Ellipse

(x-h)^2/a^2+(y-k)^2/b^2=1

a>b>0 under conventional semi-major-axis notation.

c^2=a^2-b^2, e=c/a<1.

## Hyperbola

(x-h)^2/a^2-(y-k)^2/b^2=1

c^2=a^2+b^2, e=c/a>1.

Horizontal asymptotes: y-k=±(b/a)(x-h).

## High-Risk Traps

1. Confusing radius r with r^2.
2. Losing signs when identifying the centre.
3. Treating vertical slope as zero.
4. Using equal slopes for perpendicular lines.
5. Applying ellipse notation a>b as a universal hyperbola rule.
6. Confusing c^2=a^2-b^2 (ellipse) with c^2=a^2+b^2 (hyperbola).
