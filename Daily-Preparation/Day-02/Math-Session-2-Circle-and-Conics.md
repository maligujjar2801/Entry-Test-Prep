## Circle

Standard form: (x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2

Centre = (h,k), radius = r.

For an equation with x^2 and y^2 terms, convert to standard form before extracting the radius.

## Parabola

(y-k)^2 = 4a(x-h): vertex (h,k), focus (h+a,k), directrix x=h-a.

(x-h)^2 = 4a(y-k): vertex (h,k), focus (h,k+a), directrix y=k-a.

Latus rectum length = 4|a|.

## Ellipse

(x-h)^2/a^2 + (y-k)^2/b^2 = 1.

Conventional notation uses a as the semi-major axis, so a>b>0.

c^2 = a^2-b^2 and e=c/a<1.

## Hyperbola

(x-h)^2/a^2 - (y-k)^2/b^2 = 1.

c^2 = a^2+b^2 and e=c/a>1.

Horizontal asymptotes: y-k = ±(b/a)(x-h).

For a vertical hyperbola, the squared-variable roles switch.

## Entry-Test Recognition

- Equal positive squared terms → circle after normalization.
- Sum of positive squared terms with different denominators → ellipse.
- One squared variable → parabola.
- Difference of squared terms → hyperbola.
